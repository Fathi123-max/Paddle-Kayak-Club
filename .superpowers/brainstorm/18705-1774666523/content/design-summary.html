Design Summary - Full Creative Package

📦 What We're Building

A fully immersive, scroll-driven creative experience for TSF DXB that combines:

  • 🌊 Three.js Water Hero - Animated particle waves with mouse interaction
  • 📜 GSAP Scroll Animations - Progress bar, horizontal scroll, counters, parallax
  • ✨ Framer Motion Polish - Hover glows, scale effects, staggered reveals

🏗️ Architecture

Phase 1: Three.js Hero Component
New component: HeroParticles.tsx + shader utilities
Phase 2: GSAP Scroll System
Custom hook: useScrollAnimations.ts + section integrations
Phase 3: Framer Motion Enhancements
Update existing components with motion variants
